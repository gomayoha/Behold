The Twelve

Ordinary men.
An extraordinary call.

Tap a face to walk with him — from the day he was called to the day he gave his life.

Into all the world

They went
everywhere.

From Spain to India, from the Black Sea to Ethiopia — the roads the apostles walked, as Scripture and early tradition remember them.

    The cost of following

    Ten of the Twelve
    gave their lives.

    John died old, in exile and in peace. Judas died by his own hand. The rest, as far as history remembers, followed their Lord all the way.

    Apart from James and Judas, whose deaths Scripture records, these accounts come from early church tradition. Details vary between sources.
