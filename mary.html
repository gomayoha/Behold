Her story

A young woman
said yes to God.

She held Him first, and she held Him last. No one knew Jesus longer, or loved Him more. This is her life — what she said, what she carried, and the prayer that remembers it bead by bead.

Her words.

A garland of roses

The story
of the Rosary.

The word means “rose garden.” It began with pebbles in the desert and became the prayer of saints and grandmothers, soldiers and children, in every language on earth.

    Twenty windows onto the Gospel

    The Mysteries.

    Each decade of the Rosary dwells on one moment in the life of Jesus and Mary. Traditionally a different set is prayed on each day of the week.
