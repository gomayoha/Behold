What is a saint?

Someone the light
shines through.

Asked what a saint is, a child once looked up at the stained-glass windows of her church and answered, “Someone the light shines through.” The saints were not born better than us. They let God’s light through — cracks and all.

I

Forgiven, not flawless

Augustine chased pleasure for years. Peter denied Christ. The saints are the proof that your past does not get the last word.

II

Surrendered, not superhuman

They were afraid, ill, tired and misunderstood. What set them apart was the yes they kept saying — on the bad days too.

III

Called — like you

Scripture addresses every believer as “called to be saints.” The canonised are not a separate class; they are the brothers and sisters who went ahead.Romans 1:7

“There is only one sadness: not to be a saint.”Léon Bloy

How the Church names a saint

  1. Servant of GodA cause is opened, usually at least five years after death, and their life and writings are examined.
  2. VenerableThe Pope declares that they lived the Christian virtues to a heroic degree.
  3. BlessedA miracle through their prayers is confirmed — or, for a martyr, a death for the faith. They may be honoured locally.
  4. SaintA second miracle, and the Church declares that they are with God. Their name enters the calendar of the whole Church.

For the first thousand years there was no such process: martyrs and holy men and women were honoured by the people who had known them, with their bishop’s blessing. The first saint proclaimed by a pope was Ulrich of Augsburg, in 993.

An old custom

Let a saint
choose you.

Many Christians begin the year by drawing a saint’s name, and asking that saint to walk with them until the next. Pause, say a short prayer, and draw.
